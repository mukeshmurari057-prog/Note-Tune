[Verse 1]
The water cycle moves water around Earth,
The Sun heats oceans and lakes with a bright glow.
Evaporation turns liquid water to vapor,
Up it rises, cools, and away it goes!

[Chorus]
Learn it, sing it, clap along,
Every little fact belongs!

[Verse 2]
Condensation makes tiny drops in clouds,
Precipitation is rain, snow, sleet, or hail.
Collection brings water to rivers, lakes, oceans,
Then the water cycle starts again without fail!

[Bridge]
Read it, hear it, now we know,
Let the happy knowledge grow!
